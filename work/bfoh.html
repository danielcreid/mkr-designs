Building Foundations of Hope

Branding

Building Foundations of Hope is a faith-based nonprofit organization dedicated to bettering the lives of those living in poverty around the world. Through years of fundraising and community support, Building Foundations of Hope helped build a community center in Uganda that opened in July 2017 and continues to serve as a safe haven for orphans, offering support, stability, and opportunities for growth.

The logo development process explored themes of global impact and Christian symbolism, including concepts featuring figures holding the world to represent worldwide reach and a dove referencing Christian imagery.

The final logo was implemented across a range of branded materials, including t-shirts and other merchandise, helping establish a consistent visual identity for the organization.
