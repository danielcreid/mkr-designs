Hello! My name is Melanie.

I'm a graphic designer based in Nashville, Tennessee.

Most recently, I've been working with Dreamtivity, a children's publishing company. I began by creating coloring books, educational workbooks, puzzle books, and developing licensed content for brands such as Disney and Barbie.

Over time, I took responsibility for the design and formatting of the puzzle book line, including word search, sudoku, and crossword titles. I've developed themed word lists and contributed to the production of more than 100 word search books, 50 sudoku books, and 25 crossword books.

In my free time, I enjoy learning CAD programs like Rayon and SketchUp for home projects and creative planning. I also crochet blankets and stuffed animals, play video and board games, make pickles, and watch hummingbirds at my feeder.
